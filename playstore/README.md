## Text (Main store listing → Product details)

**App name:** GIFit

**Short description (≤80 chars):**
> Pick your photos, add text, and make animated GIFs — fast, private, on-device.

**Full description (≤4000 chars):**

```
GIFit turns your photos into animated GIFs — right on your device, with no account, no ads, and no internet connection required.

Pick a few photos, arrange them, fine-tune the timing, drop on some text, and export a GIF you can share anywhere. Everything happens on your phone: your photos are never uploaded to a server.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Build a GIF from your photos
Choose images straight from your gallery using Android's private photo picker — GIFit only ever sees the pictures you hand it, never your whole library.

• Arrange your frames
Drag photos into any order. Duplicate a frame, or remove one, until the sequence looks right.

• Control the timing
Set a single speed for the whole GIF, or give any individual frame its own delay — anywhere from a snappy 0.1 seconds to a slow 10-second hold.

• Add text that moves with you
Type a caption and drag it anywhere on the frame. Pinch to resize, twist to rotate, and pick a color and font to match your style. Add one caption for the whole GIF or a different line on every frame.

• Adjust each photo
Rotate, flip, and crop individual frames so everything lines up the way you want.

• Smooth it out
Add crossfade or slide transitions between photos, and choose the output quality — from a lightweight 240p all the way up to 1080p.

• Save and share
Export straight to your gallery, or share your GIF to messaging, email, and social apps with a single tap.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

GIFit has no network access at all. There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and no sign-in. Nothing you create leaves your device unless you choose to share it. The app requests no broad storage or media permissions — it uses Android's built-in photo picker so you stay in control of exactly which photos it can see.

Whether you're making a reaction GIF, a quick photo animation, a countdown, or a fun little clip to send to friends, GIFit keeps it simple, quick, and completely yours.
```
