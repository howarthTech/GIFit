# GIFit Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-08_

GIFit ("the app") is developed by Howarth Tech Solutions. This policy explains how the app
handles your information.

## Summary

GIFit does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no network
permissions and makes no network calls of any kind — everything happens entirely on your device.

## Photos

- GIFit uses the Android System Photo Picker to let you choose photos to turn into a GIF. The
  app never receives broad access to your photo library — only the specific photos you select
  are made available to it.
- Selected photos are processed only in the app's own memory and temporary cache to build the
  animated GIF preview.
- The finished GIF is saved either to your device's public Pictures/GIFit gallery folder (so you
  can find it in your gallery app) or shared directly to another app of your choosing, only when
  you tap Save or Share.
- GIFit does not upload any photo, GIF, or metadata to any server. There is no server.

## Data collection

GIFit does not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. No usage data, device
identifiers, or personal information is collected or transmitted anywhere.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it's needed |
|---|---|
| Read selected photos (via System Photo Picker) | Lets you choose which photos become GIF frames. No blanket storage/media permission is requested. |
| Write to external storage (Android 9 and below only) | Lets the app save the exported GIF to your gallery on older Android versions that require this permission for that. |

## Children's privacy

GIFit does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does
not collect information at all.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same location with a revised
